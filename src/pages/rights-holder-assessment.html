Where does your organisation sit?

The Rights Holder Effectiveness Ladder

Box Count's proprietary Rights Holder Effectiveness Ladder applies established marketing effectiveness principles to how rights holders structure and deliver commercial partnerships. Every rights holder exists somewhere along this scale.

This quick assessment identifies your organisation's current position and the specific operational, strategic, and measurement capabilities that will strengthen your effectiveness culture, and ultimately increase the long-term commercial value of your partnerships.
