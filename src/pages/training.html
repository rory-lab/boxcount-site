You know effectiveness is in demand. Knowing where to start is harder.

Sponsorship budgets are growing faster than many traditional marketing channels, and so is the scrutiny. CMOs want to know what a partnership does for the brand. CFOs want to know what it does for the business.

The rest of marketing has decades of effectiveness thinking to lean on. Sponsorship grew up around deal-making, so effectiveness comes without a manual. It can feel abstract, technical and hard to apply to the deal in front of you.

This course is the manual: one method, from objectives to renewal, that you can use the week you get back.

After two days, you’ll be able to

  • Set objectives for a sponsorship in the business language CMOs and CFOs already use
  • Build rights and activation around those objectives, rather than an inventory list priced on a media rate card
  • Apply the evidence of what works to creative and channel planning
  • Design measurement that answers the objective: brand tracking, incrementality testing and econometrics, without falling back on AVE
  • Make the case for investment or renewal on commercial contribution rather than media exposure
  • Talk about sponsorship the way the rest of marketing talks about effectiveness
